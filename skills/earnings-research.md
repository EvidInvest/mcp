# Earnings Research Skill

Use this before a company reports earnings to build a complete picture.

## Example Prompt
---
NVDA reports earnings next week. Help me prepare:

1. get_earnings_estimates("NVDA") — what is the consensus EPS and revenue estimate?
2. get_dcf_valuation("NVDA") — what is our fair value vs current price?
3. get_pe_ratios("NVDA") — how does current PE compare to the 5-year average?
4. get_growth_rates("NVDA") — what has revenue CAGR been? Is the market pricing in continued growth?
5. get_fair_value_range("NVDA") — what is the bull/base/bear scenario?

Based on this:
- What does NVDA need to report to justify the current valuation?
- If they beat estimates by 10%, what is the upside to fair value?
- If they miss by 10%, what is the downside?
