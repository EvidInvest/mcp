# Earnings Research Example

Before an earnings report, paste this prompt to Claude:

---

NVDA reports earnings next week. Help me prepare:

1. Get the current DCF fair value for NVDA
2. Get the earnings estimates (EPS and revenue consensus)  
3. Get the PE ratio history for NVDA vs semiconductor industry
4. Get the revenue CAGR over the last 5 years
5. Based on all this, what is the margin of safety at the current price?
